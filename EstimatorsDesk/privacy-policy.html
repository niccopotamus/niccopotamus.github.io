EstimatorsDesk Pin Publisher

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EstimatorsDesk handles information in connection with its website and the EstimatorsDesk Pin Publisher app, a business tool used to create and manage EstimatorsDesk Pins through Pinterest.

Effective September 23, 2026Last updated September 23, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the EstimatorsDesk website and EstimatorsDesk Pin Publisher (the “Service”). The Service is operated by EstimatorsDesk and is used to prepare and publish EstimatorsDesk marketing content to Pinterest.

This policy does not govern Pinterest’s or Etsy’s own collection and use of information. Those services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Website information

The EstimatorsDesk website does not provide account registration, payment processing, advertising trackers, or analytics. Product purchases and customer messages are handled through Etsy. Information submitted to Etsy is processed under Etsy’s privacy practices.

3. Information the Service processes

When EstimatorsDesk Pin Publisher is connected to Pinterest, it may process only the information needed to perform authorized publishing tasks, including:

  • Pinterest account and board names or identifiers returned by Pinterest;
  • Pin content chosen for publication, including images, titles, descriptions, destination links, and board selections;
  • OAuth authorization credentials, such as access and refresh tokens; and
  • Limited technical records needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot publishing requests.
The Service does not request or receive Pinterest passwords. Authorization takes place through Pinterest’s OAuth process, and access is limited to the permissions approved by the account holder.

4. How information is used

Information is used to connect the authorized Pinterest account, retrieve available boards, publish or manage requested Pins, maintain the connection, prevent misuse, and diagnose errors. EstimatorsDesk does not sell personal information, use Pinterest data for targeted advertising, or build profiles about Pinterest users.

5. How information is shared

EstimatorsDesk does not sell or rent personal information. Information is transmitted to Pinterest only as needed to carry out an authorized publishing request. Limited technical information may be processed by service providers that support operation and security of the Service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the Service.

Pin content selected for publication becomes visible according to the destination board’s Pinterest settings.

6. Retention and security

Authorization credentials are retained only while reasonably needed to keep the approved account connection working, unless they expire or access is revoked sooner. Board information is retrieved as needed and is not maintained as a separate long-term profile. Limited operational records may be kept for a reasonable period for security and troubleshooting.

EstimatorsDesk uses reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

The connected account holder can revoke the Service’s Pinterest access at any time through Pinterest account settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access, but it does not automatically remove Pins already published to Pinterest.

You may also request that EstimatorsDesk disconnect the integration or delete information held by the Service, subject to legal, security, and fraud-prevention retention requirements.

8. Children’s privacy

The website and Service are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 13. EstimatorsDesk does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, Service, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is posted.

10. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns about this policy can be directed to EstimatorsDesk through the business’s official Etsy shop messaging channel.
